HOW TO MOVE Wallrun: jump at a red wall (or any building side) at an angle while holding W. Jump again to hop off.
Running: you start at a jog and keep picking up speed the longer you run, with no top speed. Sharp turns cost speed.
Momentum: speed you build up (running, wallruns, slides, slams, dashes) carries over as long as you keep moving. Letting go of the keys, crouching or a hard landing kills it.
Stamina: dash and air jump share one meter. It refills after a short pause, and landing a roll gives a chunk back.
Dash: press Shift to dash the way you're moving. Each dash costs one notch of stamina.
Air jump: press Space again in the air to jump, as many times as your stamina allows (one notch each).
Vault / mantle: run or jump into a ledge while holding W; you keep your speed.
Slide-hop / bhop: jump the instant you land to skip ground friction. Air-strafe (A/D + mouse) to gain speed.
Slam: Ctrl in the air. Jump right after landing for a slam jump; the higher the fall, the bigger the jump.
Roll: tap Ctrl just before a big landing to keep your speed instead of eating a hard landing.
Dash jump: jump during a ground dash to launch at dash speed.
Runner vision: red beams, wallrun panels and crates mark useful routes; cyan hooks are grapple points.
